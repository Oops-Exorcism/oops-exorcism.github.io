Privacy

When you sign up on this site, we collect only your email address.

We use it to send you news about Oops Exorcism: development updates, playtest invites and release announcements. We don't sell or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Emails are sent through Kit (kit.com), which stores the address on our behalf. You'll receive a confirmation email first, and you're only added after you confirm.

Every email has an unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to delete your address at any time by replying to any of our emails.
